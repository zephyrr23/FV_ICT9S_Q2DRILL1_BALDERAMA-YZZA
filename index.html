Beginners guide to Volleyball



Basic Rules

  • 6 players on the court at one time.
  • Maximum of 3 touches (hits) for each team.
  • Players must rotate one position clockwise every time their team wins the serve back from the opponent.
  • A ball is "in" if it hits any part of the boundary line.
